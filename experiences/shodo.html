Shodō (Japanese Calligraphy)

Japanese calligraphy workshop focusing on brush control, balance, and character rhythm.

About This Experience

Shodō, the way of writing, is a meditative art form that combines brush technique with spiritual practice. Learn the fundamental principles of Japanese calligraphy while developing focus, patience, and aesthetic appreciation.
